ABOUT MOVIERANK

Your movies. Your ranking.

MovieRank combines current movie information with a personal ranking system so users can discover movies and organize their favorites in one place.

How it works

The Movies page uses the TMDB API to display popular movies and allow users to search for specific titles.

Users can select a movie and add it to their personal ranking.

Each movie can be moved up or down, assigned a category, and given a personal rating out of ten.
FUTURE ROADMAP

Automatic recommendations

A future version could analyze a user's categories and ratings to recommend movies that match their interests.
